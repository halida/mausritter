# The Earldom of Ek


**1: Oaksgrove**

**To w n** of 350 mice. Built between
**three old oaks** linked with bridges.
Inhabitants wear fine embroidered
clothing and have a pigeon rider’s
roost.

**2: Ant kingdoms**

Three **ant kingdoms** , constantly at
war. Will pay well for mercenaries
and assassins.

**3: Logging fields**

Fields of sawdust and **felled trees**.
Sounds of **screaming machines**
can be heard during the day from
neighboring hexes.

**4: Cave of Gormenghast**

An ancient, **foul-natured wolf**.
Avoided by wise mice.

**5: Ghost beech**

Bone-white tree, translucent leaves.
**Beetle graveyard** amongst the roots.

**6: Poppyseed house**

Mouse **roadhouse**. River Rat smugglers
have **secret hideout** in the basement.

**7: Fishing spot**

3-in-6 chance of **humans** being here.
**Stones** useful for crossing river.

```
14: Tower of Magnolia
Mouse wizard’s tower , built in light-
ning-struck beech. She has almost
finished the creation of a powerful
spell, needs a cat’s claw to finish it.
```
```
15: Stumpsville
Hamlet of 50 mice. Known for their
excellent cheese , which is guarded
by a large snake. Mice have all been
kidnapped by Deadrat gang of rats.
The players start here.
```
```
16: Menhir Mot
Ancient castle , built into a large
boulder on a windswept field.
Controlled by Larkspur , lord of this
county within the Earldom.
```

```
mushrooms. Rival adventurers are
exploring the cave, searching for the
```
